Filename notations

Crystal structure filenames have the following structure: modelCation-conf_method_tag1_tag2..., where 'model' means

  • emptystring external data,
  • layer single layer in open boundary conditions,
  • cryst1 crystal with one layer per unit cell,
  • cryst2 crystal with two layers per unit cell;
'Cation' is the name of the cation as listed here; 'conf' labels conformers and polymorphs; 'method' is either a DFT method or citation to an article for external data; 'tags' are interpreted as follows:
  • L2 canonically oriented geometry in the "double" cell, that is cell containing two B-atoms (usually it is the minimal cell),
  • L4 canonically oriented geometry in the "quadruple" cell, that is cell containing four B-atoms (minimal supercell containing no direct interaction with translation images),
  • L21,L41 same as L2,L4 but for a single layer in open boundary conditions,
  • AZ external data structure has been modified by A.Z. to make it consistent accross this database,
  • 125K,275K cell measurement temperature.
